# 📊 LANTERN — DATASHEET

This datasheet documents the datasets used and generated by the **LANTERN** (Local Allocation of Network headroom for Thermal Electrification and Retrofit in Neighbourhoods) notebooks, following **Gebru et al. (2021), "Datasheets for Datasets."** LANTERN creates no primary data: every input is published by a third party or comes from the author's earlier studies, and every output is derived. One row per raw file, with version, download date, licence, resolution and the notebooks that use it, is kept in `docs/data_provenance.md`; the expected folder and file pattern of each file are in `config/data_manifest.csv`.

# 🌐 Dataset 1 — Boundaries and classifications

| Content | Source | Version | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local authority districts | ONS Open Geography Portal | May 2025 (BGC) | OGL |
| Lower-layer super output areas | ONS Open Geography Portal | December 2021, V5 (BGC) | OGL |
| Output areas | ONS Open Geography Portal | 2021, V2 (BGC) | OGL |
| Rural-urban classification of output areas | ONS | 2021 | OGL |

**Purpose.** To select core councils, assign Census and LSOA statistics to primary substations, apply the urban inclusion rule and draw LSOA maps.

**Composition.** Generalised boundary polygons and one classification per output area.

**Preprocessing.** Output areas are placed in the primary that contains their representative point; LSOAs are dissolved from output areas for mapping, so that only their urban part is coloured (`ADR-013`, `ADR-038`).

**Uses and limits.** Generalised boundaries are adequate at LSOA scale; they are not used for building-level assignment, which uses building and address points (Dataset 3).

**Distribution.** Not redistributed; available from the ONS Open Geography Portal.

# ⚡ Dataset 2 — Electricity network data

| Content | Source | Version | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firm capacity, observed maximum load, transformer ratings, planned works (LTDS Appendices 4, 5, 9) | Northern Powergrid Open Data | 2024/25 observed | NPG Open Data Licence v1.0 |
| Heat-map table: published demand headroom, accepted demand and upstream substation at every level | Northern Powergrid Open Data | as downloaded | NPG Open Data Licence v1.0 |
| Primary supply-area polygons | Northern Powergrid heat map | as downloaded | NPG Open Data Licence v1.0 |
| Network Opportunity Map headroom; primary locations | NGED Connected Data Portal | as downloaded | NGED open data |
| LTDS tables (firm capacity, demand, transformers, planned works) | NGED Connected Data Portal | Table 3: 2025; Tables 6–7: May 2026 | NGED open data |
| Primary, BSP and GSP supply-area polygons | NGED / Open Net Zero | as downloaded | NGED shared licence, **local use only** |
| Primary transformer flows, half-hourly | NGED Connected Data Portal, East Midlands | 4 December 2020 – 31 March 2021 | NGED open data |
| Distribution future energy scenarios | NGED DFES; Northern Powergrid DFES 2024 | as published | NGED open data; OGL |
| GB primary substation dataset | Zhou, Essayeh and Morstyn (2024) | v1.0.2, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10736516 | GPL-3.0 |

**Purpose.** To estimate primary and deployable headroom, the shape of existing demand on cold days, and the upstream substations that limit each primary.

**Composition.** One row per substation per operator table; half-hourly flows per NGED primary transformer; supply-area polygons. Values are operators' planning data and published headroom calculations.

**Collection and vintage.** Downloaded from the operators' open-data portals; the vintage of each table is recorded in `config/study.yaml` and `docs/data_provenance.md`. Operators update these tables regularly, so headroom values change between releases.

**Preprocessing.** Substations are matched by name or number across tables; Northern Powergrid MVA are converted to MW at a power factor of 0.95; NGED LTDS transformer rows are used as a cross-check only (`ADR-015`). Upstream limits are allocated as in `ADR-016`.

**Missing information.** Northern Powergrid does not publish primary transformer flows, and one NGED primary has no published flows; these primaries use the mean shape of the published primaries (`ADR-026`). NGED does not publish grid supply point headroom in the Network Opportunity Map. Secondary substations and low-voltage feeders are not included in any dataset used.

**Uses and limits.** Published headroom is an operator calculation at one date, not a guarantee of capacity. The published flows cover a winter that includes a lockdown period.

**Distribution.** Not redistributed. NGED supply-area polygons are licensed for local use only; maps drawn on them are not published (`ADR-008`).

# 🏠 Dataset 3 — Buildings, addresses and energy performance certificates

| Content | Source | Version | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building footprints and attributes (use, type, storeys, gross area, address count, centroid) | Verisk UKBuildings | Edition 16 | Verisk licence, **local use only** |
| Address points | Ordnance Survey OS Open UPRN | September 2026 | OGL |
| Domestic energy performance certificates, all dates | MHCLG, get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk | to latest month at download | OGL |

**Purpose.** To count dwellings in each primary, classify them into archetypes and estimate the share heated by mains gas.

**Composition.** Verisk: one row per building, extracted by location with crop polygons covering the candidate primaries (and separately for one primary on the operator boundary). EPC: one row per certificate for the core councils and their neighbours. OS Open UPRN: one point per address.

**Preprocessing.** Duplicated buildings from overlapping extracts are removed; EPC records are cleaned and the latest certificate per UPRN kept where needed; EPC and Verisk records are located by UPRN and assigned to supply areas. Archetypes and dwellings are derived as in `ADR-009`–`ADR-012`.

**Missing information.** EPC covers only part of the stock and varies by tenure and area. At a few primaries Verisk lacks residential attributes; these primaries are filled from EPC (`ADR-012`). EPC and Verisk use different construction age bands.

**Uses and limits.** Building-level attributes are used only within the pipeline; outputs are aggregated to primary, archetype, LSOA or city.

**Distribution.** Verisk data and all building- or address-level files derived from them are **not distributed** (`ADR-008`). EPC and OS Open UPRN are not redistributed; they are available from their publishers.

| Content | Source | Version | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|

| Household size (TS017), by LSOA | ONS Census 2021 | 2021 | OGL |

**Purpose.** To check Verisk counts against Census households, set hot-water occupancy, describe tenure and heating, and scale the national pathway and benchmark to the study areas by dwelling share.

**Distribution.** Not redistributed; available from ONS and the national statistics publishers.

# 🌞 Dataset 7 — Weather, solar and load profiles

| Content | Source | Version | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typical meteorological years (TMYx), Watnall and Doncaster Sheffield | Climate.OneBuilding.org | TMYx 2011–2025 (Sheffield record 2011–2022) | Open |
| Central England daily mean temperature (HadCET) | Met Office Hadley Centre | 1772–2026 | OGL |
| PV yield, annual and hourly (SARAH3) | PVGIS, EU Joint Research Centre | 2026 tool; hourly 2005–2023 | PVGIS |
| Average profiling data, Profile Class 1 | Elexon | 2013/14 | Elexon |

**Purpose.** To transfer heating profiles to local weather and set design temperatures; to select cold days for the field-trial and substation data; to estimate PV generation and self-consumption.

**Composition.** Hourly typical-year weather with statistics files; daily temperature series; PV generation per kWp; half-hourly settlement profiles.

**Uses and limits.** The Sheffield station is lower than the city and its record ends in 2022 (`ADR-014`). Typical years do not represent extreme winters; colder design temperatures are sensitivities.

**Distribution.** Not redistributed; available from their publishers.

# 🔌 Dataset 8 — Heat-pump field trials and deployment

| Content | Source | Version | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Electrification of Heat demonstration: half-hourly performance and property data | Energy Systems Catapult / DESNZ, UK Data Service SN 9209 | 2020–2023 | OGL / UK Data Service |
| Renewable Heat Premium Payment monitoring, Sample B2 | DECC / RAPID-HPC, UK Data Service SN 8151 | 2013–2015 | UK Data Service End User Licence |
| Performance reports (RHPP, Electrification of Heat) | DECC; Energy Systems Catapult | 2016; December 2024 | OGL; ESC |
| In-situ performance of metered air-source installations | Meek and Newman (2026), IEA Heat Pump Conference | 2026 | Paper |
| Heat-pump deployment statistics (MCS retrofit) | DESNZ | 2026 Q2 | OGL |
| Certified installations by council | MCS data dashboard | as downloaded | MCS |

**Purpose.** To estimate coincidence factors, check them against a second trial, set the range of heat-pump efficiency in the screening, and compare modelled installations with observed deployment.

**Composition.** Half-hourly or two-minute electricity and heat by participating home; site and property metadata; summary statistics of seasonal performance; installation counts by period and region.

**Preprocessing.** Air-source homes are selected; cold days are identified; groups of homes are drawn at random for each group size and day (`ADR-025`). RHPP energy columns are matched without regard to case; half-hours need at least 14 of 15 readings.

**Missing information.** RHPP sites have no location or outdoor temperature, so cold days are taken from HadCET. No local-authority series of annual installations is published.

**Uses and limits.** Trial homes are volunteers and may not represent the study stock.

**Distribution.** Not redistributed; access to the trial data is through the UK Data Service under its licence conditions.

# 📉 Dataset 9 — Deprivation and fuel poverty

| Content | Source | Version | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| English Indices of Deprivation 2025, File 7 (scores, ranks, deciles) | MHCLG | IoD 2025 (2021 LSOAs) | OGL |
| Sub-regional fuel poverty, Table 4 (LSOA) | DESNZ | 2026 release, 2024 data | OGL |

**Purpose.** To define deprivation and fuel-poverty groups, rank LSOAs for the concentration index and the bivariate statistics, and check the calibration against deprivation.

**Composition.** One row per LSOA: IMD score, rank and decile; households and households in fuel poverty.

**Preprocessing.** National IMD quintiles are formed from deciles (deciles 1–2 = most deprived quintile). Fuel-poor households are estimated as the LSOA share applied to every home in the LSOA (`ADR-035`).

**Uses and limits.** Both are area-level modelled indicators; they do not describe individual households and are subject to ecological inference.

**Distribution.** Not redistributed; available from MHCLG and DESNZ.

The `outputs/tables/` and `outputs/figures/` folders hold only tables and figures aggregated to primary substation, archetype, LSOA or city. The README maps every numbered table and figure to its file.

## Constraints carried into every derived table

Homes within headroom are primary-level capacity potential under the modelled design-day demand and coincidence; they are not confirmed connections, and secondary substations and low-voltage feeders are not modelled. Deployable headroom depends on a proportional allocation of upstream headroom, which is a modelling heuristic. Reinforcement dates and the installation allowance are scenarios. Waiting times are modelled intervals between desired and scheduled conversion, not observed household experience. Group and area outcomes are weighted mixtures of primary–archetype cells based on area-level indicators. Carbon differences between strategies are not programme costs.

# 📌 Cross-cutting notes

**Study scope.**

Two English urban areas defined by primary substation supply areas: Sheffield (Northern Powergrid primaries and one NGED primary) and Greater Nottingham (NGED primaries), from 2025 to 2050. Only homes heated by mains gas form the rollout pool. Competing new load (electric vehicles, new homes) is out of scope.

**Personal data.**

The repository contains no personal data. Address-level and building-level records are used only on the local machine and are aggregated before any output leaves it.

**Licensing.**

Code under the MIT Licence; the derived tables and figures in `outputs/` under CC BY 4.0. Verisk UKBuildings and NGED primary supply-area polygons are licensed for local use only. All other inputs are third-party data under the licences listed in `docs/data_provenance.md` and are not redistributed.

**Versioning.**

Network tables, EPC and consumption statistics are updated by their publishers. New releases are added as new files with their vintage recorded in `docs/data_provenance.md` and `config/study.yaml`; input files are not edited in place. A change of input requires re-running the affected notebooks (`ADR-005`).
